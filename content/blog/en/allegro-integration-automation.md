## Automation is the key to scaling

If you sell on Allegro, you know how time-consuming manual parcel management is. Copying tracking numbers, updating statuses, answering customer questions — all this takes hours you could spend growing your business.

### How does PacTrack integrate with Allegro?

The integration uses the official Allegro API with OAuth 2.0 authorization. After connecting your account:

1. **Automatic order import** — new orders appear in the PacTrack dashboard
2. **Status synchronization** — status changes in PacTrack automatically update Allegro
3. **Tracking numbers** — automatic assignment of tracking numbers to orders
4. **Notifications** — customers receive parcel status information

### Setup in 3 steps

1. Log in to PacTrack
2. Go to Settings → Integrations → Allegro
3. Click "Connect account" and authorize access

That's it! From now on, orders will sync automatically.

### Data security

We use end-to-end encryption and the official Allegro API. Your data is secure, and access tokens are stored in Azure Key Vault.
