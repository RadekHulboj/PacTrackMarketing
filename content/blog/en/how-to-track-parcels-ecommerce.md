## Why is parcel tracking crucial?

In today's e-commerce, customers expect full transparency in the delivery process. Research shows that **93% of customers** want to know where their package is at any given moment.

### Benefits of a professional tracking system

1. **Fewer customer support inquiries** — customers check the status themselves
2. **Greater trust** — transparency builds loyalty
3. **Fewer complaints** — delivery proof protects the seller
4. **Better reviews** — quick information = happy customer
